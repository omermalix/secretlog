What is Secretlog?

Secretlog is a private personal vlog app designed for people who want to capture the real, unfiltered texture of their daily life. Record a 2-second clip every hour. The app compiles them automatically into a beautiful mini vlog at the end of the day.

Your videos are stored only on your device and in your own personal Google Drive. We have no servers, no data collection, and no social feed. Everything belongs to you.

Your Privacy Matters

Secretlog is built around privacy. We do not collect your data, we do not run ads, and we do not share anything about you with anyone. Your videos are yours alone.
